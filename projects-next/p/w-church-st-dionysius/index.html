Church St. Dionysius

Contemporary repainting or conservation of the existing building? This was the decision facing the congregation of the Roman Catholic parish church of St. Dionysius in Elsen, a district of Paderborn. As part of the extensive renovation of the listed church building, the decision was made in favour of an artistic re-painting by the internationally renowned artist Tobias Kammerer from Rottweil, thus bringing contemporary art into the church rooms.
