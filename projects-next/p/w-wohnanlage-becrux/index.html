Residential Complex Becrux

An avant-garde residential complex has been built on one of Madrid's busiest streets. Its curved lines of fibreglass-reinforced concrete create a friendly and unique facade that, together with the volume of the adjacent tower, forms a complex visible from afar just a stone's throw from the centre of the capital.
