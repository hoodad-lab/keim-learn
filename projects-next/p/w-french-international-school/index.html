French International School

In Hong Kong's Tseung Kwan O district, sunlight hits the kaleidoscopic facade of the new French International School campus and penetrates the building through windows laid over a grid of 627 colourful tiles. This puts the 19,600-square-metre new school, designed by Henning Larsen Architects, at the forefront of educational innovation in Hong Kong.
