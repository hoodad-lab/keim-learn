Holland House

The surviving east wing and arcades of this Grade I listed 17th-century house in Holland Park, now a youth hostel, were restored. A KEIM Royalan system with a Restauro Lasur glaze gave the west arcade a stone effect, and Royalan covered the soffits and east wing.
