Royal Brisbane Hospital (Brisbane General Hospital Precinct)

The hospital opened on Bowen Bridge Road, Herston, in 1867, and by the end of the 1930s it was the largest hospital complex in Australia. Its historic precinct, which includes the 1875 Fever Ward and the 1895 Lady Norman Wing, was added to the Queensland Heritage Register in 2003.
