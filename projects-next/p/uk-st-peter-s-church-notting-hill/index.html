St Peter's Church, Notting Hill

Thomas Allom's Grade II* church of 1855-57, said to be London's last 19th-century classical church, was fully restored. A layered KEIM Soldalit and Restauro Lasur scheme gave the facades a subtle limewash-like Bath stone tone.
