Evelyn Grace Academy

Evelyn Grace Academy is a secondary school in Brixton run by the ARK academy trust, with a 100 m running track that passes through the middle of the building. Designed by Zaha Hadid Architects, it opened in 2010 and won the RIBA Stirling Prize in 2011. KEIM listed it as one of its worldwide references.
