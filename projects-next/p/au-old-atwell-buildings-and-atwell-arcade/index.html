Atwell Buildings and Atwell Arcade

Atwell Arcade and the heritage-listed Atwell Buildings link High Street and Cantonment Street in Fremantle's West End. In a $13 million redevelopment of about 5,500 m², the upper floors became boutique offices and the original heritage features were kept.
