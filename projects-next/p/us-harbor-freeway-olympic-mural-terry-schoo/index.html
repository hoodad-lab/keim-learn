Harbor Freeway Olympic Mural (Terry Schoonhoven)

Working above the Harbor Freeway and using Keim mineral paint, Schoonhoven painted Roman ruins and Greek sculpture in the foreground of a mirror image of the downtown Los Angeles skyline for the 1984 Olympics freeway murals.
