Marina One

Marina One in Singapore is a special building complex: four skyscrapers form the framework of a central three-dimensional garden - for a better interaction of working, housing and living. In particular, glass, steel and certified green concrete were used for the construction. Ensuring the longevity of the concrete in humid tropical climates is a challenge for major projects of this kind. The tropical city state of Singapore is situated on the southern tip of the Malay peninsula between Malaysia and Indonesia.
