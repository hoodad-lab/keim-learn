Marina Bay Sands

Marina Bay Sands is a luxury integrated resort in Singapore that opened in 2010. The 20-hectare resort was designed by Moshe Safdie Architects, who used stacks of playing cards as a template. Located on the shores of Marina Bay, it comprises an impressive ensemble of three 55-storey hotel towers, a huge conference and exhibition centre, a shopping mall, an art and science museum, two theatres, numerous restaurants and bars, an infinity pool and a rooftop garden. The resort is a Singapore landmark and attracts millions of visitors from all over the world every year.
