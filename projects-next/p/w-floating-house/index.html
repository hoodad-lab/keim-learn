Floating House

Design by Studio F. A. Porsche – under this label, Carinthian timber construction specialist GRIFFNER presents its new two-storey luxury prefabricated house made of wood and glass. The first show house in the series, called Floating House, was built at the company's headquarters in Griffen – a sustainable, two-storey timber construction with rooms up to 5.85 metres high, enveloped by continuous facade bands.
