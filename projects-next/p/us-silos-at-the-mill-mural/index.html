Silos at the Mill Mural

Guido van Helten wrapped the H.D. Lee Flour Mill grain elevator with local children playing 'Ring Around the Rosie'. Translucent Concretal-Lasur kept the character of the aged concrete, with Concretal Black for the negative space.
