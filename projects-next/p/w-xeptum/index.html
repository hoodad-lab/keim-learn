Xeptum Consulting AG - Headquarter Place X

With the new "place X" headquarters of Xeptum Consulting AG in Neckarsulm, Anna Philipp and her team of architects have created an innovative working environment that combines cosiness and efficiency. The IT company's office building harmoniously combines the industrial and natural environment: four wings extend from a central foyer into the vineyards. Green roofs, a wooden façade and photovoltaic systems emphasise the company's ecological and sustainable approach.
