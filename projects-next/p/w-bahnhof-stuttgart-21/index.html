Stuttgart 21

The new concourse at Stuttgart's main railway station is a masterpiece of engineering. Designed by ingenhoven associates and Frei Otto, the freely curved shell roof is nearing completion: the concrete surfaces were carefully reworked to emphasise the elegance of the flowing forms. The company beconart used KEIM products for this purpose. Stuttgart's future main station forms the centrepiece of the major Stuttgart 21 project. An underground, 8-track through station will replace the existing 16-track terminus station.
