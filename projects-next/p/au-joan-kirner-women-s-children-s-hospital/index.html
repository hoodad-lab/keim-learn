Joan Kirner Women's & Children's Hospital

The new multi-storey ward building of the Joan Kirner Women's and Children's Hospital in Melbourne heralds a new approach to the design of healthcare facilities with its colourful façade. The new health-oriented design is intended to provide orientation for patients and visitors, increase well-being and reduce stress. Colour, design and architecture can support the healing of patients - this has been known for a long time. In Australia, the Joan Kirner Women's and Children's Hospital in Melbourne and the Royal Hobart Hospital in Tasmania's capital Hobart are two special hospitals.
