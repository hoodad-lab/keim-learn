Résidence Étudiante Jouvence

The Jouvence student accommodation, designed by A2A Architectes, features a contemporary and understated architectural style in the heart of a dynamically developing neighbourhood. The various building blocks are united into a single entity by a subtle gradation of mineral shades on the concrete façades. This gives the complex a distinctive yet understated identity and allows it to blend harmoniously into its urban surroundings. KEIM Concretal-Lasur was used for the project.
