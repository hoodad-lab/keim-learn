Lagom Residential Complex

Either they turn grey unattractively or they are high-maintenance: there are many prejudices against wooden facades. The Lagom residential quarter on Lake Ammersee puts a stop to this. The complex relies on a colour-stable coating system for its outer skin of wooden slats, which is not only durable and easy to maintain, but can also be applied in advance.
