Moody Amphitheater, Waterloo Greenway

The open-air theatre at the heart of the Waterloo Greenway, a steel lattice canopy over curving raw concrete. When the cast concrete came out patchy, a mineral stain strategy using Concretal-Lasur subtly camouflaged the mismatches while keeping its earthy, raw look.
