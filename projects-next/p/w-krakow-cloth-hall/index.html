Kraków Cloth Hall

The Cloth Halls are located in the centre of Krakow's main market square. It owes its name to the cloth trade, one of the most important goods sold here. It is considered one of the most important examples of Renaissance architecture in Central Europe. Today it houses a branch of the Krakow Historical Museum with an impressive collection of Polish paintings and sculptures from the 19th century. There are also numerous souvenir shops and craft stalls on the ground floor. The entire building was extensively renovated from 2009-2010. Numerous KEIM products were used for the design of the facade and interior.
