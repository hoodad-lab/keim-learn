Dengel House Reutte

The Obermarkt 3 house is undoubtedly one of the most beautiful and powerful buildings in the market town of Reutte (Tyrol). In 2020, an elaborate restoration took place. Extraordinary design competence was expected in the process. The craftsmanship and design achievements of the past became the challenge of the present. The employees of Hornstein & Co. KG Malerei und Vergoldung took on the task with respect and gave the building new splendour. The contract for the restoration of the facade went to the company Hornstein & Co. KG Malerei & Vergoldung, a Reutter family business in its 4th generation.
