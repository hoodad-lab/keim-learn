King William Charles Lunalilo's Tomb

The royal mausoleum of Hawaii's first elected king, in the grounds of Kawaiaha'o Church. After restoration the precast stone was given two coats of Royalan colour-matched to the original, and photos 3.5 years later show it still kept clean by rainfall alone.
