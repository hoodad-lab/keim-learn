Headquarters Josef Rädlinger

New company headquarters for the internationally active Josef Raedlinger group near Cham in the Bavarian Forest. A new three-storey building in monolithic solid brick construction relates to the neighbouring former Windischbergerdorf monastery, which was renovated and converted in parallel. In order not to dominate the monastery building too much, the building was partially modelled into the sloping terrain .For the exterior and interior of the building solely ecological and durable materials were used.
