Grand Hotel Łódź

The Grand Hotel in Łódź, a vibrant university and industrial city in Poland, has been the epitome of prestige and elegance for 135 years. Following a comprehensive renovation in 2023, it now harmoniously combines historical splendour with state-of-the-art five-star comfort. With 152 luxurious rooms and flats, two exquisite restaurants, an elegant café and unique conference rooms, including an impressive historic theatre hall, the hotel meets the highest standards.
