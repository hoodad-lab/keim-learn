Homes & apartments · Neugablonz, Germany

Iser-quartier

A newly built cooperative residential quarter in the Allgäu region is making a name for itself: With its architecture, its special colour scheme and the smallest details, the extremely well thought-out settlement refers both to its bohemian past and to its surroundings. The final touch is given to the quarter with high-quality colour products. Even the welcome sign in the entrance area of the new Iser Quarter in Neugablonz, Bavaria, makes it clear: something special has been created here: Each of the houses bears the name of a personality who comes from the Reichenberg region in former northern Bohemia.
