Cultural and Congress Centre Jordanki

The Jordanki Cultural and Congress Centre lies like an embedded rock in Poland's medieval UNESCO World Heritage city of Toruń. Due to a particularly roughly worked concrete structure, the building complex looks like a cave both inside and out. The building was designed by the Spanish architect Fernando Menis. With its brick interior, it makes reference to the nearby old town of Toruń. The building has already received several awards. Among others, it was considered one of the 7 new Polish wonders by National Geographic.
