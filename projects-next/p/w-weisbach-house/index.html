Weisbach House

The Weisbach House in Plauen, one of the last surviving Baroque manufactory buildings in Saxony, was a ruin after the Second World War. After initial repairs in the 1990s, the city of Plauen decided to completely renovate the building in 2015 so that the ‘Factory of Threads’, a museum that brings together the collections from the Plauen Lace Museum and the Vogtland Museum, could move in. After restoring the outer structure of the building, the late baroque plaster façade with its yellow-ochre colour scheme was reconstructed. This also included painting the façade with KEIM Soldalit façade paint.
