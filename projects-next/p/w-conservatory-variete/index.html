Conservatory Varieté

The traditional Wintergarten Varieté Berlin, long known for spectacular productions and big names in show business history, is now also making an impressive architectural statement: extraordinary linear LED lighting bathes the striking façade of the 19th-century building in atmospheric light in the evening hours – warm, inviting and a real eye-catcher. Lighting solution from Barthelme LED Solutions, specialising in customised linear LED lighting solutions for architecture, design and industry.
