Juliette Gordon Low Birthplace

The 1821 birthplace of the Girl Scouts' founder, Savannah's first National Historic Landmark. Hard cement stucco that was damaging the soft brick was removed and replaced with mesh-reinforced Universalputz lime stucco, finished in two coats of Soldalit, with PSS 20 anti-graffiti on the west wall, aiming for a 100-year solution.
