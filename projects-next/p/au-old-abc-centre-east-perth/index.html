ABC Centre, East Perth

In 2005 the ABC moved its Perth operations from the 1960 studios on Adelaide Terrace into a newly built studio complex in East Perth, at Fielder Street near Claisebrook. The building houses ABC radio, television and newsroom operations for Western Australia.
