The Leaning Woman statue

Karel Vogel's Grade II listed 1938 concrete statue by the Great West Road had cracked and rusted. It was repaired and finished in KEIM Granital, unveiled by the Duke of Gloucester in 2024 and given a Hammersmith Society award.
