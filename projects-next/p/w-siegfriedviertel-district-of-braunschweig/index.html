Heritage · Braunschweig, Germany

Siegfriedviertel District of Braunschweig

The energy-efficient modernisation of a listed facade using historical plastering techniques is a demanding task that requires preserving the beauty of the facade while combining it with the useful. The gatehouse of the listed building complex in Braunschweig's Nordstadt district, which spans several streets, was to be fitted with an ETIC system as a pilot project. The plaster facades from the 1930s were finished with a special comb-pull technique, which had to be reproduced on the surface of the thermal insulation. At the beginning of planning, surface techniques on ETIC systems were still little known.
