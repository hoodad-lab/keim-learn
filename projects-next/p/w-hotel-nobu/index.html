Hotel Nobu

What do the former second largest building in Europe and a hotel by Robert de Niro have in common? The design with concrete! On the facade of one building and in the interior of the other, this material plays a striking role. Exposed concrete once had a bad reputation, but today it is impossible to imagine the world without it. Its natural grey colour conveys a certain elegance and timelessness, but it can also be coloured. The Hotel Nobu in Warsaw presents itself in a completely different way: it combines a historical building with a newly built, modern wing.
