Victorian College of the Arts Secondary School

The Victorian College of the Arts Secondary School is a select-entry government school for young dancers and musicians. Its purpose-built two-storey Miles Street facility of about 5,460 m2, designed by Williams Boag with sprung-floor dance studios and acoustic music rooms, was completed in June 2009.
