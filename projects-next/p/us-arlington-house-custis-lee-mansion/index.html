Arlington House (Custis-Lee Mansion)

The 1802-1818 Greek Revival mansion in Arlington National Cemetery, now a National Park Service memorial. After mock-ups and NPS approval the stucco was stripped, repaired and skimmed with Universalputz, and the historic faux ashlar finishes on the facades and columns were recreated with KEIM mineral coatings.
