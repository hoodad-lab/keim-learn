How the ratio works

Concretal-Lasur is the colour; Concretal-Fixativ is clear. Mix more Fixativ in and the colour becomes a light, translucent glaze that lets the concrete's own texture show. Less Fixativ, and it moves toward full colour. Slide the ratio and drag the line across the wall to compare.
